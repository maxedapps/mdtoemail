## On making room

I have noticed that good ideas rarely arrive while I am actively looking for them. They tend to appear during walks, while cooking, or in the few quiet minutes before the day properly begins.

> Perhaps attention needs empty space in the same way that a room needs windows.

This week, I am trying to leave more things unfinished—not abandoned, but open long enough for something unexpected to enter.
